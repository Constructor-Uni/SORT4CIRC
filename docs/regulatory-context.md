# Regulatory context and limits

This educational profile helps developers explore identifiers, product records, provenance and exchange. It does not determine which obligations apply to a product or organisation.

"Normative" describes only the public-profile rules versioned here. Public-profile conformance is distinct from legal/regulatory conformity. The repository is not an official European Union specification, European Commission certification or CEN/CENELEC certification. It provides no legal advice, proof of ESPR conformity or proof of a deployment's legal compliance.

Evaluate applicable requirements independently with appropriate expertise. References and vocabulary labels are contextual information, not an assessment of completeness or applicability.
