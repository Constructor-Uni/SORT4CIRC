# Glossary

- **DPP:** a structured product record accessible through an identifier.
- **Carrier:** a representation of an identifier, such as a fictional QR-code payload.
- **Observation:** an attributed claim with method, status and time.
- **Lifecycle event:** an attributed occurrence recorded separately from product characteristics.
- **Projection:** a selected view of a record.
- **Digest:** a hash of a defined byte representation.
- **Integrity receipt:** a backend's submission-status record; the supplied backend is a mock.
- **Public-profile conformance:** agreement with this repository's versioned rules, distinct from legal conformity.
